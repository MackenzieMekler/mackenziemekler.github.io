# About Me   
# Howdy!    
I'm somephdstudent, Mack. My research interests lie at the intersections of cancer biology and immunology. My thesis project involves studying the properties of multiple myeloma cells that enable them to resist CAR T cell treatment. I am pursuing a PhD with the end goals of becoming a professor and PI in the fields of immunology and oncology.    
## What am I doing here?    
I started this blog with a handful of things in mind:    
1. To improve my skills in science communication. A reason for my staying in academia comes from a love for teaching and discussing science. I hope to use this platform to practice this skillset and prepare for my career.    
2. As a platform to talk with the public. We have entered a time of mistrust of science and I believe that to move past this, scientists will have to find ways to explain what we do to the public without barriers or condescension. I would like to take a step forwards in doing that here.    
3. As a platform to talk with scientists. Whether it be my research, development, or special topics within science, there are a number of things I would like to discuss with my colleagues and I hope that this will serve as a platform to do just that. I hope to discuss broad scientific topics like statistics, data analysis/visualization, and scientific research related to my field.
